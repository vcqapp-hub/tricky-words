Frequently asked questions

What is Tricky Words?

Tricky Words is an Android word-search puzzle where players connect letters to uncover hidden words across handcrafted grids.

Can I play Tricky Words offline?

Yes. Campaign levels, daily challenges and dictionary content are designed for offline play.

How do hints work?

Hint coins can reveal a next-letter coordinate when a puzzle becomes difficult.

Who is Tricky Words for?

The game is designed for word-search fans, vocabulary lovers and people who enjoy casual brain puzzles.
